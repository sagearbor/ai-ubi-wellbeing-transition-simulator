What “less error” measures

It is the reduction in average absolute prediction error against keeping each country at its starting value. Wellbeing and income are scored separately; there is no combined score.

2025 wellbeing: (0.3890 − 0.3513) ÷ 0.3890 = 9.7% less error.

Those are cohort-wide errors in 0–10 ladder points, not percentages of happiness. A 20% reduction would require MAE below 0.3112 on the same endpoint test. Future tests could improve or worsen the score.

What the new experiment found

No breakthrough this round. Removing health slightly improved this specification. The tested diminishing-income response weakened overall predictions. None beat the existing country-offset model or reached a 20% endpoint gain.

How the bold line was chosen

The default uses 75% country offsets (#24) and 25% Claude’s decaying offsets (#29). This was the lowest 2025 wellbeing error among the earlier 583-recipe catalog. Its 10.2% improvement over persistence was selected after the outcomes were known. It is the historical catalog winner, not a demonstrated best future forecast.

The 50/50 alternative uses the same two models with equal weights. The new four-model average uses exactly 25% of each matched diagnostic variant; these weights were frozen before their new score. That round’s model forms were still designed after earlier results on this period were known. None is a fresh independent confirmation.

Blending predicted values differs from averaging their errors. One model’s high forecast can cancel another’s low forecast. That helps only when their errors complement each other, and this relationship can change. Forecast combinations reference.

What the future lines assume

All forward lines begin at observed 2025 wellbeing and income. They use unchanged fits and country trends estimated from 2015–2018. Country offsets run the original forecasting function; the decaying-offset model recomputes its gap from the 2025 origin. The health variants keep their earlier health and income trends. Governance remains the old background value. No new AI shock, bill, tax, or transfer policy is applied.

This is an illustration of what those frozen models imply from the newer starting point. It is not an updated operational forecast. No future accuracy has been scored. Historical variants without a checked forward adapter are unavailable in the future view; no line is extended by eye.

The spread between models is not calibrated uncertainty. A weather-style probability cone would require a probabilistic model and evidence that its stated coverage actually holds. These related models alone cannot supply that evidence.

The diagnostic was frozen before its new score

A matched two-by-two comparison switches health on/off and log income versus a saturating income transform. All variants share GDP extrapolation, 0.8 trend damping, a fixed 0.1 ridge penalty, the same training window, and the same target masks. The income-saturation scale is the training-cutoff median GDP, not a value optimized on the test period.

The original health entry is reconstructed as the control. Removing health refits its companion income coefficient, so this is a matched feature-removal check. It does not prove anything about the causal effect of health. The full protocol and predictions were committed before the single new score; the scoring command refuses an existing receipt.

These tests answer narrow diagnostic questions. They reuse an already-known period, so the word “independent” applies to the implementation checks, not to fresh predictive evidence. Internal rolling-origin checks use only observations earlier than each fold origin. Time-series evaluation reference.

Source data, missingness, and reproducibility

Historical observations and forecasts come from the repository’s frozen evaluation artifacts, not freshly downloaded outcomes. The survey target is a trailing three-year average; adjacent years overlap. Source definition. The 2025-origin paths exclude countries missing either endpoint; no older value is silently substituted.

Projection adapters must reproduce the saved 2018 forecast before generating a new origin. The original country-offset implementation reproduces exactly; the health-control and decay reconstructions match within floating-point tolerance. The original frozen experiment files remain unchanged.

To rebuild paths without rerunning the scored experiment, use the supported Node runtime and Python 3:

node --import tsx scripts/evaluation/forecast-paths/forward-offset.ts
python3 scripts/evaluation/forecast-paths/build_paths.py
python3 scripts/evaluation/forecast-paths/render_paths.py

Do not rerun score_once.py. Results are already published. The chart generator only reads those artifacts and constructs the displayed paths. The chart data carry the experiment provenance.
