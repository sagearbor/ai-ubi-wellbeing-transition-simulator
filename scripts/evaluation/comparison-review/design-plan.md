# Comparison report design

Audience: Sage and the other implementation lead. Primary job: choose the next forecasting experiment without confusing a retrospectively selected blend with fresh evidence.

Palette: white #ffffff; cool paper #f4f6fc; ink #182b4d; indigo #344ebc; good #176b49; bad #ae3548. Dark mode reverses surfaces and lightens text, keeping green/red meaning stable.
Typography: Avenir Next (local) for display and body, system sans fallback; tabular numerals for comparisons. No remote fonts/assets.
Layout: left-aligned executive recommendation, a three-column comparison of individual/blend/evidence status, then a full-width interactive square matrix. Controls operate all quantitative views. Deep audit material stays in collapsed details.

    Recommendation              Why no production switch yet
    Individual   50/50 blend    Search winner
    [Target] [Endpoint / all years]
    Singles table
    Pair matrix                 Selected blend detail
    Complete finite catalog
    Audit / weak spots / next actions

Distinctive element: the clickable blend matrix, including bad combinations and original/persistence baselines. Avoid a decorative hero chart or identical card grid; the report's visual structure follows the actual decision. Large metrics appear only where their denominators and comparison are explicit.

Review against brief: a generic leaderboard would hide target and horizon tradeoffs. Revised plan keeps scope controls visible and shows gain over the best ingredient next to gain over persistence. No future-tense improvement claims. Browser-check mobile overflow, keyboard controls, theme, and CSV export.
