It does not relicense third-party dependencies, datasets, reports, paper text, trademarks, or externally hosted assets. Attribution is not a substitute for permission where permission is required.

## Dependencies

Dependencies retain their own licenses and notices. Exact versions are in `package-lock.json`; installed package notices are distributed with those packages. Preserve relevant notices when redistributing a build. This project does not claim ownership of React, Vite, Gemini SDK, Firebase, mathematical/visualization libraries, or other dependencies.

## Historical and country data

Original providers include the World Bank (World Development Indicators and Worldwide Governance Indicators), World Happiness Report/Gallup, and Our World in Data. Per-dataset source links, retrieval dates, indicator definitions and revisions are retained in `data/provenance/`, `data/countries/`, `data/hindcast/` and `data/evaluation/level-holdout-2018/`. Do not infer that every third-party series shares the project software license. Follow the original provider's dataset-specific reuse conditions when redistributing or publishing analyses. Revised vintages and provider restrictions remain applicable.

## Company financial statements

`data/financials/` contains attributed numerical observations and short line-item descriptions from company reports, with source URLs and reporting periods. Company reports and names remain the property/identifiers of their original owners. No affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement is implied. Global consolidated figures do not measure AI-only proceeds or a particular country's earnings.

## Research models and policy text

Each model port and historical policy case cites its original paper in `docs/design/research/` and corresponding data/model sources. The project's equation implementations do not transfer ownership of the underlying paper or authorize copying its full text. Source policy text in worked examples and bundles retains its original provenance; user imports may have different rights. Cite original authors separately from this software.

## Release checklist

Before a journal/archive submission, the maintainer must confirm a complete distribution-specific third-party license inventory, final human author list, and dataset reuse conditions for the deposited contents. This notice records boundaries; it does not claim a completed legal audit or grant rights held by someone else.
