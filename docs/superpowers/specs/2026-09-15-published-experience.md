# Published-data experience

User direction: build the best new version on a separate GitHub branch, grounded in published Apple and other organization financials, visibly compare modeled history with observations, keep model/variable/policy authoring discoverable, and provide a portable Claude review prompt. The owner explicitly rejects an anonymous hypothetical AI dividend fund as the starting point.

## Product

A new front door asks **What could a share of a company's cash flow fund?** Choose a real company, inspect its annual cash-flow statement, allocate a policy share between resident payments and training, then see what money, training capacity and job openings constrain. Compare two editable scenarios. A first-class **Check against history** page shows actual observations, model reconstruction, a simple persistence baseline and misses. Model Lab, policy text translation, imported equations, uncertainty, world maps and AI risk remain available. This is one application and one generic core model runner: the new budget is an authored CoreModel, not another bespoke simulation engine.

The new entry is a single-period allocation experiment based on a filed fiscal year. Its monthly payment is the annual allocation divided by twelve, not a forecast or observed monthly cash flow. Advanced world trajectories keep their existing model and qualification. The new result must never masquerade as that old world result. Opening exact equations uses the exact current CoreModel and existing Lab bundle mechanism. Ordinary navigation must preserve the experiment and the mounted Lab. New model/financial results do not inherit old qualification badges.

## Evidence and arithmetic

Use a pinned, documented first collection of at least six separately consolidated publicly reporting companies including Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta and Nvidia, all USD fiscal annual reports (prefer FY2025). Exact period dates, filing/report URL, line-item units, retrieval date and source evidence excerpts belong with each observation. Record operating cash flow, cash PP&E purchases, revenue, net income, and dividends/buybacks where verified. Missing items are null, not zero. Private firms and subsidiaries are not fabricated or separately counted. Say collection coverage, not all AI companies.

The experiment's derived annual base is operating cash flow minus cash PP&E purchases. State this exact definition; exclude acquisition payments, lease-financed investment etc unless included in the reported PP&E cash item. It is before dividends and buybacks and is not idle cash or measured AI-generated cash. A proposed allocation may redirect existing uses. A negative difference stays visible; the allocation ceiling is explicitly max(0, difference). Nominal USD per financial period is preserved. The experiment does not combine unlike fiscal periods or nominal company amounts with constant-2015 country GDP. Select one company per experiment. Recipient population is a pinned, sourced resident count with its own observation year; rates and training costs are scenario assumptions. A reported financial field cannot be edited into another 'reported' value.

The core model defines source ceiling, proposed budget share, dividend/training split, training cost per completion, instructor capacity, eligible trainees, placement rate and suitable openings. Actual training spend is capped by capacity and budget; unused training money is visible and is not silently diverted to dividends. Gross placements are limited by openings and completions; no net jobs, causal treatment effect or wellbeing improvement is inferred. All constraints and cash identities use executable invariants. Extreme inputs fail or show explicit binding limits. Exactly the same CoreModel goes to the browser worker and Lab export.

## Historical page

Generate a versioned small artifact from the existing hindcast harness, holding all existing published targets fixed. Show annual actual/model/persistence series by country for wellbeing and GDP; include sample counts, exclusions, errors and a data table/download. Label this **historical reconstruction** because the anchor used the same 2015–2025 observations; it is not a held-out forecast or validation of the new cash-allocation model. Clearly say AI/UBI are off in the headline run, and this is the legacy world model. Include the full cohort aggregate, observed time window, fit window, model identity, source hashes and reproducible command. Avoid a misleading GDP correlation when modeled growth is effectively identical across countries. Show the model's shortcomings upfront. Offer the sensitivity runs without cherry-picking the best. Do not fit or tune anything to make this page look good. Future holdout evaluation is a named missing capability, not an implied completed claim.

## Interaction and appearance

One memorable composition: a legible cash-flow waterfall beside a compact policy control panel, changing in response to the user. White/soft blue-gray surfaces (#ffffff, #eef3f8), dark blue ink (#172d48), royal blue action (#285acb), copper comparison (#b45b27), teal observed series (#16766a). System humanist sans (Avenir Next, Segoe UI, sans-serif); generous 40–56px headlines, readable 16px body, tabular numeric alignment. Dark mode has explicit high-contrast counterparts. No decorative gradient, canned hype, tiny uppercase labels or wall of cards. The time basis and source link are adjacent to the main figure. A short boundary statement stays visible, detail opens on demand.

Desktop layout: header Explore / Compare / Check against history / Model Lab; main heading and short question; company+fiscal year source strip; left policy controls, right financial waterfall and outcomes; direct next actions; advanced capabilities below. Mobile stacks without horizontal page overflow, keeps controls and outcomes close, uses native accessible elements, visible focus, reduced-motion support, dark/light screenshots. Comparison has two real independent editable scenarios with matched financial source and cohort. Share links are bounded, versioned, validate all inputs, preserve A/B and active view, and fail explicitly on invalid or stale identifiers. A downloadable exact model and scenario remains available.

## Global constraints

- Preserve old share/hash owners and model results. Never silently overwrite a mounted Lab draft.
- Reuse the existing core engine and worker runner; do not change the legacy economic engine or its qualification source manifest to implement the new experiment.
- Never change published-paper targets, historical observations or existing qualification evidence to obtain a passing check.
- Financial observations, derived quantities and policy assumptions must be distinguishable beside the results.
- No API secrets, new paid services, cloud deployment or merge. Publish a separate review branch and draft PR.
- Implementers own their assigned files, are not alone in the codebase, and must not revert others' work. No worker-spawned subagents.

## Alternatives considered

Keeping the hypothetical fund violates the owner's direction. Relabeling market capitalization as cash flow is wrong. Replacing every legacy source and re-pinning old review evidence would destroy the comparison and hide a new modeling assumption. The selected design uses the existing general-purpose CoreModel runner for a new, explicit, sourced allocation model, while preserving the other supported model families and their evidence boundaries. Six audited groups are preferable to seventy-nine unsourced organizations; adding another report is a data contribution with the same schema.
