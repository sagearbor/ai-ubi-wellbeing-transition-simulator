**employment_rate → `alaska-pfd-calibration`.** reproduction of the authors' own calibration, compared with their estimate. Fitted to this outcome: no.
Independence: Not independent: the same authors published the calibration beside the estimate and revised its inputs between editions (MPC 0.25 -> 0.5, kappa 1.2 -> 0.9, per-person -> per-household PDV). No parameter here was chosen by this repository.
Applicability: Small (7% of labour income), resource-funded, one state, not countercyclical.
Alternatives: default -0.7 pp; NBER w24312 (Jan 2020) calibration -0.2 pp.

**employment_rate → world engine `evidence-anchored`: mechanism-absent.** The world engine has no labour-supply or local-demand response to transfers; the check verifies that by running it, rather than asserting it. A zero here is a missing mechanism, not a prediction of zero. Transfer in the check run: 0.79% of labour income (the case's dose: 7.25%).

Limits:
- Small transfer: the authors say the effect of UBI-sized payments (e.g. USD 1,000/month) is uncertain (AEJ p.337).
- Resource-rent funded: no tax-side distortions are in the estimate.
- The general-equilibrium channel is not directly tested (AEJ p.316).
- Average over 33 years with a 5-year pre-period; the part-time effect is near zero in 1982-85 and grows.
- Not a micro elasticity: within-Alaska designs find negative hours effects (Feinberg & Kuehn 2018); different estimand.
