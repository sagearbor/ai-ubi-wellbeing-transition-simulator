## New numbered findings

### N1. Critical — residual scaling still manufactures a root; the precision guard also rejects a legitimate one

**Location:** [engine.ts:783](https://github.com/sagearbor/ai-ubi-wellbeing-transition-simulator/blob/436a16e0ea81c7744037c0e34813bcae925c2ffc/src/core/engine.ts#L783), [engine.ts:808](https://github.com/sagearbor/ai-ubi-wellbeing-transition-simulator/blob/436a16e0ea81c7744037c0e34813bcae925c2ffc/src/core/engine.ts#L808), [engine.ts:811](https://github.com/sagearbor/ai-ubi-wellbeing-transition-simulator/blob/436a16e0ea81c7744037c0e34813bcae925c2ffc/src/core/engine.ts#L811).

The acceptance threshold is `residualTol ?? Math.max(tol, 1e-9 * scale0)`, where `scale0` is taken from the bracket endpoints. The comment says a jump or pole “is rejected.”

**Failure:** use residual `(1e12*(u-0.5)^2 + 1) * (u < 0.5 ? -1 : 1)` on [0,1], with default tolerances. This function has **no zero**: its absolute residual is always at least 1. The engine returns **ok:true**, u=0.4999999990686774, residual=−1.000000867361738, no diagnostics. Large endpoint values make an economically unnormalized residual of 1 acceptable.

Conversely, `1e12*(u-1e-15)` on [0,1] with residualTol=1e-9 has an exactly evaluable root at 1e-15. The engine stops near 8.88e-16 and calls it a discontinuity. `4*EPSILON*max(1,abs(mid))` is not the local spacing near zero, and stops refinement prematurely.

**Minimal fix:** use separately defined absolute/relative residual tolerances in a declared residual normalization, independent of arbitrary bracket endpoints. Detect actual inability to form a new midpoint rather than using a unit-scale epsilon floor. Report numerical nonconvergence when continuity is unknown; do not claim to have proved a discontinuity. Require both probes and the three original cases to pass.
