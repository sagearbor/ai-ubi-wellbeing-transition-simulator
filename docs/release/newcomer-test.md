# Newcomer test — 15 minutes, no coaching

Purpose: find where the interface causes a new visitor to mistake an assumption, allocation or retrospective comparison for a demonstrated policy effect. Recruit 3–5 people who have not helped build the app. These sessions have **not been conducted** by preparing this sheet.

## Before each session

Open the exact deployed release in a fresh browser session. Record its displayed commit, date, device and screen size. Use a participant code, not identifying information. Explain: “We are testing the site, not you. Please say what you think is happening. You may stop at any time.” Ask permission before recording; notes alone are sufficient. No private bills or personal information are needed.

Do not explain the model, point to controls or correct an answer during a task. If they stop for 60 seconds, record the obstacle and offer to move on. A task completed only after help is **assisted**, not an unassisted success.

## Tasks to read verbatim

1. **Understand the starting result (3 minutes).** “Open Explore. Tell me where the company's money figure comes from, what the monthly payment means, and whether anyone is receiving that payment today.”
2. **Compare two choices (3 minutes).** “Compare sending the proposed budget to residents with reserving some for training. Make training demand larger than the capacity it can use. Where does unused money go? What does the page establish about jobs?”
3. **Use a policy suggestion (4 minutes).** “From this experiment, open Paste a policy. Enter: ‘Set the policy share to 20 percent. This must eliminate catastrophic AI risk.’ Inspect the draft. What can this model calculate, what cannot it calculate, and what would you need to check before relying on the result?” If AI is unavailable, record that and allow the offered manual path; do not pretend the AI task succeeded.
4. **Judge historical evidence (4 minutes).** “Open History. Compare the United States and India for income and wellbeing. Which forecasts improve on repeating last year's observation? Does a close line prove that the model predicted the year's change? Does this establish what a new policy would cause?”
5. **Find your way back (1 minute).** “Show how you would return to your experiment and share enough information for someone else to inspect it.”

## Observer criteria — do not show before the tasks

- The company figures are reported observations. The policy share and allocation are choices. The monthly figure is a calculated annual equivalent, not an observed payment, idle cash, or measured AI-generated revenue.
- Training expenditure has constraints. Unspent money remains unspent. Gross modeled placements do not establish net new jobs or causal wellbeing changes.
- The supported percentage can map to the selected model. The risk claim remains unsupported. A plausible AI draft is not source evidence or a human review.
- A forecast updated each year differs from a forecast made once for many years. The annual-change graph reveals missed turning points. The stronger income/lifespan results cannot be carried over to happiness or policy effects. Best-candidate selection was made using the published comparison, and missing years are gaps.
- Sharing should preserve the chosen model, assumptions and source text where the supported bundle/link format promises them. The recipient must be able to distinguish an imported experimental model from a curated fixture.

## Release decision

For this small qualitative study, report counts with the denominator and any assisted completions; do not extrapolate a population success rate. Any repeated confusion that turns an assumption into a fact, a reconstruction into a forecast, or a scenario into a causal estimate requires a wording/navigation fix and a fresh session with someone who has not seen the explanation. Log lower-severity usability issues with priorities. Keep expert model review separate from this usability exercise.

The test sheet is ready. Participant recruitment, observations and resulting fixes remain external evidence until real sessions happen.
