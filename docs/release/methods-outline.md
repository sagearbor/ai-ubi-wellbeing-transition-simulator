# Methods preprint outline — research draft

No author list, affiliations, DOI, publication date, acceptance or new empirical success is asserted by this outline.

1. Research need: expose economic assumptions and constraints for AI-transition resource choices; explain what existing modelling tools already provide and why this interface adds value.
2. Supported model families: explicit stepping, bounded equilibrium solving and independent model adapters. Explain unsupported intertemporal optimization and why an imported equation is not automatically compatible.
3. Reproducibility contract: source/model identity, units, timing, constraints, seeded draws, worker limits, replay bundles and fail-closed provenance checks.
4. Published financial illustration: audited cash-flow source definitions, global consolidation, recipient choice, funding ceiling, training constraints and visible unspent funds. Separate accounting from investment, tax, behavioural and wellbeing effects not estimated.
5. Model reproduction: equation-level paper ports and independent expected values; distinguish faithful reproduction, fitted calibration and outside-model comparisons.
6. Historical evaluation: existing reconstruction plus preregistered retrospective holdout; disclose revised data vintages, post-period model design, train/test boundary, origin cohort, missingness, persistence baseline and the primary worse-than-persistence wellbeing result.
7. Policy translation: quoted settings versus evidence for response coefficients, unsupported clauses, unit conversion, conflicting mappings and human review boundaries.
8. Limitations and failure examples: solver assumptions, sensitivity, capped responses, incompleteness, noncausal placement outputs and lack of blind external forecasting evidence.
9. Evaluation of usability and adoption: report actual observed studies only after they occur, with methods and appropriate permission. Do not fabricate user feedback or claim expert review from automated reviewer agents.
10. Reproducible availability: exact released version, archive DOI after assignment, code/data licensing and human accountability for AI-assisted development.
